// BOARD POST : 11/08/2026

// 'as real as i can possibly be'


ever since Kat found out we're a system, this weird sense of guilt has been hanging over our head like a sword on a string.

like... we're faking it all. just making it all up for attention. it's strange really! how on earth would we be faking if i'm writing this bulletin post right now?


unfortunately, just because it's easy to prove doesn't make it any easier to stop feeling like a fraud...

i hope one day we'll get there. i hope one day we don't have to worry about what other people think! i like to keep my chin up about things.

i can't believe there's people out there in the world that gain pleasure from being mean to others...

// 'like staring into a mirror'


it's always interesting to think about how similar i am to Kat. at some point, i was nearly 1:1 to her.

i like how similar we are though. it just means that we don't have to fight to spend time in front! at least not as much as others might... <\3

it's nice being... alive. getting to be myself and finding out what makes me feel like me, has been so much fun so far!

i still like what Kat likes, and i see her almost as a sister to me! i'm not sure if that's weird or anything... but we're just so similar and we get along well. she likes what i like, and i like what she likes!


it all works out to make things really nice i think.
